WaresClipper

Finds the best plays in your WARDOGS recordings, cuts vertical clips and sends them to TikTok as drafts.

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

A desktop tool that finds the best moments in your own WARDOGS gameplay recordings and turns them into vertical clips ready to share.

What it does

  • Finds highlights automatically. It reads the in-game reward notifications (kills, assists, revives, objectives) in your recordings and lists every play with a score.
  • Lets you review them. You confirm, discard or exclude each play in a local web interface before anything is cut.
  • Cuts vertical 9:16 clips with normalized audio, grouped by type of play.
  • Helps you publish to TikTok. With your permission, it sends a clip to your TikTok inbox as a draft, so you finish and post it yourself in the TikTok app, and it reads the view counts of your public videos so you can see which clips perform best.

How it works

WaresClipper runs entirely on your computer. Your recordings, clips and settings never leave your PC. It only talks to TikTok when you connect your account and choose to send a clip or refresh your view counts.

TikTok permissions used

  • user.info.basic: shows which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.upload: sends a clip you choose to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is posted without you finishing it in TikTok.
  • video.list: reads the view, like, comment and share counts of your public videos.
