Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

WaresClipper is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

1. Data that stays on your computer

Your gameplay recordings, the clips WaresClipper creates, your reviews, notes and settings are stored only in folders on your computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by WaresClipper. WaresClipper does not operate any server, does not have user accounts, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

2. Data from TikTok

If you choose to connect your TikTok account, WaresClipper uses TikTok Login Kit and receives, with your permission:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload access (video.upload): used only to send clips that you select to your TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Your public videos' information (video.list): video IDs, links, descriptions, creation dates and view, like, comment and share counts, used only to show the performance of your clips inside the application.

The access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, and the information listed above, are stored only on your computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.

3. Data sent to TikTok

WaresClipper sends data to TikTok only when you take an action: the video files you choose to send as drafts, and the requests needed to sign in and to read your video statistics. TikTok's handling of that data is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

4. The sign-in redirect page

This website hosts a page that TikTok redirects to after you sign in. That page only forwards the one-time authorization code to the WaresClipper application running on your own computer (address 127.0.0.1). It does not store, log or transmit that code anywhere else. This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's own privacy statement.

5. Deleting your data

You can disconnect your TikTok account in WaresClipper at any time, which deletes the stored tokens from your computer. You can also revoke WaresClipper's access from your TikTok account settings. Deleting the WaresClipper folder removes all of its data from your computer.

6. Children

WaresClipper is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
